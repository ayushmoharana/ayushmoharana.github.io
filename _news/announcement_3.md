We found that V446 Cep, a {\beta} Cep pulsating binary is actually a higher-order multiple! The work is published in the [Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society.](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/548/4/stag772/8661527).

---

The system has a 10.68 solar mass pulsator and a 1.657 solar mass star in a close binary of 3.8 days. This binary is again orbited by a 4.11 solar mass companion, that is invisible in the spectra or multiband photometry. We therefore, expect it to be either a black hole or a quadruple system of smaller masses.
